Definitions & assumptions. Install rate (had-a-chance, the default) = installed ÷ (installed + canceled + pendings already past their install date) — an order scheduled beyond the report date hasn’t had its chance yet and doesn’t count either way. Resolved basis = installed ÷ (installed + canceled). All-sold basis = installed ÷ every account sold. Past-pending = still pending and past its order due date (Brightspeed) or scheduled activation date (Verizon/Frontier). Brightspeed “abandoned” rows carry no order number and are excluded — they are dead leads, not sold accounts. Verizon/Frontier rows are product lines and are rolled up to one record per account number, anchored on the fiber data line. Verizon and Frontier are treated as one company. Pay tiers: <70% → $10, 70–75% → $15, ≥75% → $20 per installed account; commission figures assume payout applies to installed accounts. The Verizon/Frontier export includes no street addresses on fiber orders, so the heat map covers Brightspeed accounts (plus the few Verizon mobile sales that carry a zip). The exports weren’t pulled live, so “past” is judged against each report’s own as-of date, inferred from the latest activity in the file: Brightspeed , Verizon/Frontier . Reporting window: June 1 onward, except Brightspeed which is intentionally limited to orders created July 1 onward. Weeks start Monday; the latest week is partial.
